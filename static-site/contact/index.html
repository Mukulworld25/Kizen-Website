Common Questions

Quick answers before you reach out

Can my child start ACCA directly after 12th?

Yes — 12th pass with English and Mathematics/Statistics is sufficient to begin, though many students choose to complete B.Com or BBA first.

Do you offer weekend batches?

Yes, weekend and evening batches are available for working professionals across all postgraduate and ACCA programmes.

Is there a fee for the counselling session?

No, initial counselling and pathway planning is completely free.

Are fees payable in installments?

Yes, flexible installment plans are available for all programmes. Our counsellors will walk you through the options during your visit.

How large are the batches?

Batches are intentionally small so faculty can track every student’s progress individually — that’s core to how we teach.

Can we try a class before enrolling?

Yes, we invite prospective students to attend a trial class and visit the campus before making any commitment.
