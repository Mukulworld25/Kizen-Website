About Kizen Education

We built the only institute that doesn't say goodbye at graduation.

Kizen Education was created in Sector 34-A out of a deep passion for teaching and a simple observation: commerce students kept changing institutes at every stage. We built a permanent academic home to fix that.

Our Founding Story

One address, one philosophy, seven years of a student's education.

Kizen Education was founded on the conviction that switching coaching centres between school, graduation and professional credentials fractures learning. Each transition meant new faculty, disjointed methods, and lost momentum.

At Kizen, our core philosophy rejects rote memorisation. We focus on conceptual clarity, real-world finance applications, and ethical student care. The institute was built as a safe, inspiring learning space where quality education and dedicated mentorship come first.

Today, from SCO 193-195 in Sector 34-A, Chandigarh, we deliver an uninterrupted curriculum spanning Class 11-12 Commerce, B.Com, BBA, M.Com, MBA, ACCA, and emerging Finance Technology certifications.

How We Teach

Concept-driven, small batches, seamless transitions — four principles that guide every class.

Concept-driven, not rote

Every topic is taught from first principles so students can solve unfamiliar problems, not just memorise patterns.

Small batches by design

Capped class sizes mean faculty track individual paper-wise progress and intervene early when a student struggles.

Seamless stage transitions

Faculty who teach Class 11 also teach ACCA — the same academic relationship continues, eliminating re-learning gaps.

Mock exams every cycle

Timed, full-length practice mapped to actual board/university/ACCA exam sessions with detailed feedback.

Three principles that shape every classroom decision

01
Continuity over convenience

We'd rather build a 7-year relationship with a family than optimise for a single semester's enrolment.

02
Global standard, local access

A qualification recognised in 180+ countries shouldn't require leaving Chandigarh to prepare for.

03
Small batches, real mentorship

Every stage keeps class sizes small enough for faculty to know each student by name and progress.
